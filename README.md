## About Me

I am a **Biomedical Engineering researcher** working at the intersection of **artificial intelligence, computational toxicology, bioinformatics, chemoinformatics, and drug discovery**.

My work focuses on developing **interpretable, reproducible, and applicability-domain-aware machine learning and deep learning models** for toxicity prediction, QSAR, molecular representation learning, drug safety assessment, and biomedical systems modeling.

I am currently pursuing a **Master of Biomedical Engineering (M.Eng.)** at **Kumoh National Institute of Technology, South Korea**, where my research includes computational pharmacology, bioinformatics, deep learning, QSAR, and biomedical modeling.

My research philosophy is simple:

> **A high-performing model is useful only when we also understand when, why, and for which samples it can be trusted.**

## ToxCML — Hybrid mfCoQ-RASAR

**Consensus QSAR + Read-Across for Multi-Endpoint Toxicity Assessment**

[![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/Repository-View%20Code-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/kit-cml/ToxCML-Hybrid-mfCoQ-RASAR)
[![Paper](https://img.shields.io/badge/Paper-View%20Publication-2563EB?style=flat-square)](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00357)
![Quartile](https://img.shields.io/badge/Journal%20Quartile-Q1-15803D?style=flat-square)

A hybrid toxicity-prediction platform integrating **consensus QSAR** and **similarity-based read-across** for comprehensive multi-endpoint chemical safety assessment.

**Highlights**
- 18 toxicity endpoints
- 54,601 chemicals
- Consensus QSAR and read-across
- Random Forest, XGBoost, and SVM
- Multiple molecular representations
- Scaffold-aware validation
- Applicability-domain assessment
- SHAP-based interpretation

**Publication**  
**F. S. Nursyafi**, M. A. Pramudito, Y. N. Fuadah, R. N. Izza, A. L. Fauzan, K. M. Lim.  
*ToxCML: A Hybrid mfCoQ-RASAR-Based Platform Integrating Consensus QSAR and Read-Across for Comprehensive Multi-End Point Toxicity Assessment.*  
**Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling — Q1**, 66(8), 4538–4575, 2026.  
[DOI: 10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00357](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00357)

## Interpretable Multi-Modality Consensus QSAR

**Machine Learning + Deep Learning for Multi-Endpoint Toxicity Prediction**

[![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/Repository-View%20Code-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/kit-cml/QSAR-consensus-framework-study)
[![Paper](https://img.shields.io/badge/Paper-View%20Publication-2563EB?style=flat-square)](https://doi.org/10.1080/15376516.2026.2643659)
![Quartile](https://img.shields.io/badge/Journal%20Quartile-Q2-1D4ED8?style=flat-square)

An interpretable consensus QSAR framework combining **machine learning, deep learning, multiple molecular representations, chemical-space analysis, applicability-domain assessment, and explainable AI**.

**Highlights**
- 8 toxicity endpoints
- 30,160 compounds
- Morgan, MACCS, APF, and physicochemical descriptors
- Random Forest, XGBoost, SVM, and DNN
- Independent and external validation
- UMAP chemical-space analysis
- SHAP-based explainability

**Publication**  
**F. S. Nursyafi**, M. A. Pramudito, Y. N. Fuadah, K. M. Lim.  
*Interpretable Multi-Modality Consensus QSAR Framework: Integrating Machine and Deep Learning for Enhanced Multi-Endpoint Toxicity Assessment.*  
**Toxicology Mechanisms and Methods — Q2**, 2026.  
[DOI: 10.1080/15376516.2026.2643659](https://doi.org/10.1080/15376516.2026.2643659)

## Cardiosim-Tox

**Interpretable Multitask Deep Learning for Cardiac Ion-Channel Toxicity**

[![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/Repository-View%20Code-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/kit-cml/CardiosimTox_code)
[![Paper](https://img.shields.io/badge/Paper-View%20Publication-2563EB?style=flat-square)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00204-026-04519-4)
![Quartile](https://img.shields.io/badge/Journal%20Quartile-Q1-15803D?style=flat-square)

An interpretable multimodal multitask QSAR platform for predicting blockade risk and potency for major cardiac ion channels:

**hERG · Cav1.2 · Nav1.5**

**Highlights**
- Binary blocker/non-blocker classification
- pIC₅₀ potency regression
- Molecular fingerprints and descriptors
- Graph-based molecular representations
- Multitask deep learning
- Multimodal fusion
- Applicability-domain analysis
- External validation

**Publication**  
**F. S. Nursyafi**, B. Kang, J. Eom, et al.  
*Cardiosim-Tox: An Interpretable Multitask Deep Learning QSAR Platform with Multimodal Feature Fusion for Predicting hERG, Cav1.2, and Nav1.5 Blockade Risk and Potency.*  
**Archives of Toxicology — Q1**, 2026.  
[DOI: 10.1007/s00204-026-04519-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00204-026-04519-4)

## ProCMT-QSAR

**Protein-Conditioned Multimodal Multi-Task QSAR**

[![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/Repository-View%20Code-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/kit-cml/ProCMT-QSAR)
![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Manuscript%20Under%20Review-D97706?style=flat-square)

A target-aware QSAR framework for endocrine-disrupting chemical toxicity prediction using **protein-conditioned multimodal learning and multitask modeling**.

**Research components**
- Molecular multimodal representations
- Protein embeddings
- Protein-conditioned modeling
- Cross-attention
- Multitask learning
- Mixture-of-Experts fusion
- Scaffold-based validation
- Applicability-domain assessment
- Molecular-level explainability

**Manuscript**  
**F. S. Nursyafi**, K. M. Lim.  
*ProCMT-QSAR: An Interpretable Target-Aware Protein-Conditioned Multimodal Multi-Task QSAR Model for Toxicity Prediction of Endocrine-Disrupting Chemicals.*  
**Status:** Under review.
