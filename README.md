## About Me

I am a **Biomedical Engineering researcher** working at the intersection of **artificial intelligence, computational toxicology, bioinformatics, chemoinformatics, and drug discovery**.

My research focuses on developing **interpretable, reproducible, and applicability-domain-aware machine learning and deep learning frameworks** for molecular toxicity prediction, QSAR modeling, drug safety assessment, molecular representation learning, and biomedical systems modeling.

I am currently pursuing a **Master of Biomedical Engineering (M.Eng.)** at **Kumoh National Institute of Technology, South Korea**, while conducting research in computational medicine and AI-driven biomedical modeling.

I am particularly interested in building computational models that are not only accurate, but also **scientifically interpretable, externally validated, and reliable within clearly defined chemical or biological domains**.

## 1. ToxCML — Hybrid mfCoQ-RASAR

> **A Hybrid mfCoQ-RASAR-Based Platform Integrating Consensus QSAR and Read-Across for Comprehensive Multi-End Point Toxicity Assessment**

[![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/Repository-ToxCML-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/kit-cml/ToxCML-Hybrid-mfCoQ-RASAR)
[![Paper](https://img.shields.io/badge/Paper-JCIM-005A9C?style=flat-square)](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00357)
![Quartile](https://img.shields.io/badge/SCIE-Q1-success?style=flat-square)
![Year](https://img.shields.io/badge/Year-2026-informational?style=flat-square)

ToxCML is a large-scale hybrid toxicity-prediction framework integrating **consensus QSAR** and **similarity-based read-across** for comprehensive chemical safety assessment.

**Key features**
- 18 toxicity endpoints
- 54,601 chemicals
- Consensus QSAR + consensus read-across
- Random Forest, XGBoost, and SVM
- Multi-fingerprint molecular representations
- Scaffold-aware validation
- Tiered applicability-domain assessment
- SHAP-based model interpretation
- Reliability-aware prediction for toxicity screening and hazard prioritization

**Publication**

**F. S. Nursyafi**, M. A. Pramudito, Y. N. Fuadah, R. N. Izza, A. L. Fauzan, K. M. Lim.  
*ToxCML: A Hybrid mfCoQ-RASAR-Based Platform Integrating Consensus QSAR and Read-Across for Comprehensive Multi-End Point Toxicity Assessment.*  
**Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling — SCIE Q1**, 66(8), 4538–4575, 2026.  
**DOI:** [10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00357](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00357)

## 2. Interpretable Multi-Modality Consensus QSAR

> **Integrating Machine and Deep Learning for Enhanced Multi-Endpoint Toxicity Assessment**

[![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/Repository-Consensus%20QSAR-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/kit-cml/QSAR-consensus-framework-study)
[![Paper](https://img.shields.io/badge/Paper-Toxicology%20Mechanisms%20%26%20Methods-005A9C?style=flat-square)](https://doi.org/10.1080/15376516.2026.2643659)
![Quartile](https://img.shields.io/badge/SCIE-Q2-blue?style=flat-square)
![Year](https://img.shields.io/badge/Year-2026-informational?style=flat-square)

An interpretable multi-modality consensus QSAR framework combining **machine learning, deep learning, multiple molecular representations, applicability-domain assessment, and explainable AI** for multi-endpoint toxicity prediction.

**Study scope**
- 8 mechanistically distinct toxicity endpoints
- 30,160 unique compounds
- Morgan, MACCS, APF, and physicochemical descriptors
- Random Forest, XGBoost, SVM, and Deep Neural Networks
- 10-fold cross-validation
- Independent test and external validation
- SHAP-based explainability
- UMAP chemical-space analysis
- Applicability-domain assessment

**Publication**

**F. S. Nursyafi**, M. A. Pramudito, Y. N. Fuadah, K. M. Lim.  
*Interpretable Multi-Modality Consensus QSAR Framework: Integrating Machine and Deep Learning for Enhanced Multi-Endpoint Toxicity Assessment.*  
**Toxicology Mechanisms and Methods — SCIE Q2**, 2026.  
**DOI:** [10.1080/15376516.2026.2643659](https://doi.org/10.1080/15376516.2026.2643659)

## 3. Cardiosim-Tox

> **Interpretable Multitask Deep Learning QSAR for Cardiac Ion-Channel Blockade Risk and Potency**

[![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/Repository-Cardiosim--Tox-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/kit-cml/CardiosimTox_code)
[![Paper](https://img.shields.io/badge/Paper-Archives%20of%20Toxicology-005A9C?style=flat-square)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00204-026-04519-4)
![Quartile](https://img.shields.io/badge/SCIE-Q1-success?style=flat-square)
![Year](https://img.shields.io/badge/Year-2026-informational?style=flat-square)

Cardiosim-Tox is an interpretable **multimodal multitask deep-learning QSAR platform** for predicting cardiac ion-channel blockade across three major cardiac safety targets:

**hERG · Cav1.2 · Nav1.5**

The framework addresses both:
- **Binary classification** — blocker vs. non-blocker
- **Regression** — blockade potency prediction using pIC₅₀

**Core components**
- Molecular fingerprints
- Mordred molecular descriptors
- Graph-based molecular representations
- Multitask deep learning
- Multimodal feature fusion
- Chemical-space analysis
- Applicability-domain assessment
- External validation
- Interpretable QSAR modeling

**Publication**

**F. S. Nursyafi**, B. Kang, J. Eom, et al.  
*Cardiosim-Tox: An Interpretable Multitask Deep Learning QSAR Platform with Multimodal Feature Fusion for Predicting hERG, Cav1.2, and Nav1.5 Blockade Risk and Potency.*  
**Archives of Toxicology — SCIE Q1**, 2026.  
**DOI:** [10.1007/s00204-026-04519-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00204-026-04519-4)
